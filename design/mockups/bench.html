Python 3.14 is mostly about three things: template strings, a free-threaded build that is no longer experimental, and lazily evaluated annotations [1].

  • t-strings (PEP 750) look like f-strings but give you a Template, not a str [2].
  • Free threading is supported rather than a build-time experiment [3].
  • Deferred annotations stop forward references from needing quotes.
